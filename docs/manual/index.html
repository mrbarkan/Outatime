Outatime

The manual. Everything Outatime does, from the first click in the menu bar to the report you send a client at the end of the month.

      Getting started

      Download Outatime.dmg from the latest release, drag Outatime to Applications and open it. It needs macOS 26 Tahoe or later.

      Outatime has no Dock icon and no main window. It lives in the menu bar as a small clock. Click the clock to open the menu panel, where all the tracking happens.

      To have it waiting for you every morning, turn on Open at Login in Settings → General.

      Allow notifications when macOS asks. The tomato timer, the stretch reminder and the “target reached” message all arrive as notifications.

      Tracking time

      Click an activity to start it. Click it again to stop. Clicking a different activity stops the current one and starts the new one in the same moment, so your day has no gaps you didn't choose.

      The six activities

      ActivityUse it forCounts toward the target
      WorkYour normal working hours.Always
      BreakCoffee, a walk, a short pause.By default
      LunchYour meal break.If you turn it on
      ExtraTime past your daily target, and work on weekends and days off.Always
      TravelGetting to a client or a site.By default
      Out of OfficeAppointments and errands during the day.By default

      Choose what counts in Settings → Tracking. Work and Extra always count.

      Notes

      Type in the field under the status line and press Return. The note is saved on the running block, and you can add as many as you like. When nothing is running, the note goes on today's last block instead, so you can still jot down what you just finished.

      Timers left running

      A timer still running at midnight is cut there and carries on in the new day, so each day keeps its own hours. The panel warns you with “Running since…”, and after ten hours you get a “Still tracking?” notification. Change or turn off that reminder in Settings → Tracking.

      Clients

      If you work for more than one client, Outatime can keep their hours apart. Add your clients in Settings → Clients. A client picker then appears in the menu panel.

      • Pick a client and Work, Extra and Travel are tracked for them. Break, Lunch and Out of Office never belong to a client.
      • Switch clients while working and the block ends right there; a new one starts for the new client. Switch within the first minute and Outatime treats it as a correction: it relabels the block instead.
      • Remove a client and they leave the picker, but their past blocks keep their name, and you can still export their month.

      In the Logbook, each block shows its client (“Work · Acme”), and you can change it in the block editor.

      Your daily target

      Set how many hours a day you aim to work in Settings → Tracking. Outatime compares what you've done against it everywhere: in the menu panel, in the Logbook and in reports. A green number is time ahead, a red one is time behind.

      • Weekends and days off owe nothing. Mark a day off with the umbrella button in the Logbook toolbar.
      • Today never shows as behind in red. The day isn't over yet.
      • The hours bank adds up every day's balance since a date you choose, which is handy if your hours are settled each quarter or year.

      Extra after the target

      Once today's hours reach the target, a running Work block switches itself to Extra, cut at the exact minute you got there, and you get a notification. On weekends and days off, all work is Extra. Turn this off in Settings → Tracking if you prefer to switch by hand.

      The Logbook

      Open it from the menu panel. The sidebar lists the month day by day, with each day's hours, a bar of what it was made of, the month's balance and each week's total. Pick a day to see it as a timeline.

      Editing blocks

      • Blocks snap to five-minute steps and to the edges of their neighbours. Blocks that touch move together: drag a shared edge and both change.
      • Double-click empty space, or use the + button in the toolbar, to add a block. It's an hour of Work and stops at the next block rather than overlapping it.
      • In the editor, ⌥ Return starts a new line of notes, and the duration stepper moves the end in five-minute steps.
      • Delete a block from its editor.

      Templates

      When your days tend to look the same, set one up in the Logbook, then choose Templates → Save Day as Template. Apply it to any other day from the same menu. Applying a template to a day that already has blocks asks before replacing them.

      Getting around

      Use the arrows in the toolbar or ⌘ [ and ⌘ ] to move a day at a time, and ⌘ T to come back to today. Zoom the timeline with ⌘ − and ⌘ =.

      Focus and breaks

      Tomato timer

      Click the timer button at the top of the menu panel to turn it on. It starts Work and runs focus rounds of 25 minutes with 5-minute breaks, and a 15-minute break after every fourth round. The menu bar counts down the round.

      When a round ends, a notification offers Start Break or Back to Work, and switches the tracker for you. Lunch, Travel and Out of Office pause it until you're back. Change the durations in Settings → Tomato timer.

      Stretch reminder

      After 50 minutes of unbroken Work or Extra, Outatime nudges you to get up. Switching clients doesn't reset it; a break does. It stays quiet while the tomato timer is on, since the tomato's breaks already get you moving. Change the interval or turn it off in Settings → Tracking.

      Reports

      Export from the menu panel for the current month, or from the Logbook toolbar for the month you're looking at.

      ExportWhat's in it
      Monthly reportAn Excel workbook: a summary with a row per day and its balance, hours per client, and every block.
      Client reportOne client's month for invoicing: their hours per day and the blocks behind them, without targets or balances.
      Master workbookEverything since you started, opening on a dashboard of hours, balance and activity by month.
      Daily summaryA CSV with a row per day, for Numbers, Notion or anything else.
      EntriesA CSV with every block of the month.

      The monthly report's rows can be pasted under the master workbook's tables, and its dashboard picks them up. If your master workbook was made before version 1.2, export a fresh one first: blocks now have a Client column.

      Settings

      Open Settings from the ••• menu in the menu panel, or press ⌘ , while the panel is open.

      GeneralLight or dark, language (English, Español, Português), what the menu bar shows, the colored menu bar icon, Open at Login, and updates.
      TrackingThe daily target, what counts toward it, the hours bank, Extra after the target, reminders and global shortcuts.
      ClientsAdd, rename and remove clients.
      Tomato timerFocus, short break and long break durations.

      What the menu bar shows

      The running activity's icon and how long it has been going, the icon only, the time only, or how far today is from the target. The icon takes the activity's color unless you turn colored icons off.

      Updates

      Outatime checks for new versions by itself and installs them in place; you never need to visit the download page again. To check right away, use Settings → General → Check for Updates. After an update, a What's New window lists what changed.

      Beta updates

      Turn on Get beta updates in Settings → General to receive early builds of the next version. They may have rough edges. Turn it off again and you'll move back to regular releases with the next one.

      Your data

      Everything Outatime knows is in a single file on your Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere; the only time Outatime goes online is to check for updates.

      ~/Library/Containers/com.dbarkan.Outatime/Data/Library/Application Support/Outatime/data.json

      Copy it to back up your history. If the file ever can't be read, Outatime sets it aside next to the original, named data.json.broken- plus a number, and starts fresh rather than overwriting it.

      When something's off

      No notifications arrive

      Open System Settings → Notifications → Outatime and allow notifications. The tomato timer, reminders and “Daily target reached” all depend on them.

      I can't see the clock in the menu bar

      On a crowded menu bar, macOS hides items that don't fit, especially beside the camera notch. Quit an app you don't need in the menu bar, or hold ⌘ and drag the clock further right.

      A day has the wrong hours

      Open it in the Logbook and fix the blocks: drag them, resize them, or click one to type exact times. Balances and reports update straight away.
